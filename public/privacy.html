Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: August 31, 2026 · App: Kith Expense & Trip Ledger

1. Overview & Local-First Architecture

Kith ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Darpan Parmar, is a collaborative group travel expense tracker and personal finance manager. Kith is built on a local-first philosophy: your financial data, expense ledgers, and trip details are stored locally on your device by default.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you link your Google Account, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo provided by Google OAuth. For guest users, we create an anonymous guest identity without collecting personal identifiers.
  • Financial Ledger & Trip Data: Expense amounts, category tags, timestamps, trip titles, and split ratios you enter into the app.
  • Cloud Synchronization: Data is synchronized across trip members using encrypted communication with our secure backend ledger.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Calculate accurate debt simplification graphs and group balances.
  • Synchronize shared trip expenses between authorized trip members.
  • Deliver real-time push notifications when co-travelers record or settle expenses.
  • We NEVER sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption. Cloud backups and sessions are stored in enterprise-grade secured infrastructure with strictly enforced row-level security.

5. User Rights & Permanent Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your account, expenses, and cloud backups at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Profile → Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  • Web Data Deletion Portal: If you uninstalled the app, visit our Web Data Deletion Portal.
