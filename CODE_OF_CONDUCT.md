# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **Kith (SplitTrip)** project a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free, and productive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical background.

---

## Expected Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect toward other contributors.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project, its offline-first data safety, and user privacy.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and architectural decisions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassing, trolling, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Spreading malicious code, exploits, or uncoordinated security hazards.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, pull requests, issues, and other contributions that do not adhere to this Code of Conduct.

---

## Reporting & Inquiries

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at `conduct@nirniq.com`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
